Wallino Reader is an iOS app for a self-hosted Wallabag server app. If you don't host Wallabag yourself, please check out the official Wallabag iOS app, since it also offers a subscription based backend without self hosting. 

Wallino is free and open source, please check out the GitHub repository. 

Features:
- Archive news articles from websites, news apps and PDFs for archiving or reading later. You can even import plain text to create a new article entry. 
- Use the share extension from other apps: Wallino can even extract a news article link from text
- Assign tags to articles to better organise your article archive. 
- Export the original link or export the article text as PDF to share with others
- Apple Intelligence integration: automatic tagging and article summaries. 

Read the Wallabag documentation to find out more about how to set up Wallabag on your home server or rented cloud server: https://doc.wallabag.org.
