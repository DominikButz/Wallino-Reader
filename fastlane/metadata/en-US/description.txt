Wallino Reader is a an iOS app for a self-hosted Wallabag server app. If you don't host Wallabag yourself, please check out the official Wallabag iOS app, since it also offers a subscription based backend without self hosting. 

Wallino is free and open source, please check out the GitHub repository. 


Features:
- Archive news articles from websites and news apps for archiving or reading later. 
- Use the share extension from other apps: Wallino can even extract the news article link from text
- Set tags for better organisation of your reading archive
- Export the original link or export the article text as PDF to share with others

Read the Wallabag documentation to find out more about how to set up Wallabag on your home server or rented cloud server: https://doc.wallabag.org.
